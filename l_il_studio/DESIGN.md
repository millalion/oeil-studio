## Brand & Style

### Personality & Positioning
This design system embodies the rigor and poetry of an elite graphic design workshop and typographic atelier. Built as a high-precision visual training ground for visual thinkers, it marries the quiet, tactile poise of classical Swiss and French print editions with the immediate, surgical feedback of contemporary creative software. The tone is exacting, intellectual, inspiring, and calm. It flatly rejects modern SaaS mannerisms: there are no synthetic multi-stop gradients, no floating glassmorphic blurs, and no gamified confetti explosions. The tool values the practitioner’s gaze, treating visual training as an artisan craft.

### Emotional Target
The user should feel the stillness and focus of an architectural drafting room or a letterpress printshop:
- **Discipline & Clarity**: The interface steps back, prioritizing optical judgment without visual noise.
- **Intellectual Respect**: Clean typography, sharp borders, and deliberate negative space convey confidence and mature craftsmanship.
- **Tactile Curiosity**: Interactive handles, nudgers, and calibration tools feel concrete, sharp, and physically grounded.

### Design Movement: Modern Editorial Precision
The visual language operates on an axis between **Editorial Minimalism** and **Technical Utilitarianism**. Surfaces mimic premium warm paper stocks, punctuated by crisp optical white working plates, dense carbon black text, delicate structural hairlines, and single-origin chromatic accents assigned to distinct aesthetic disciplines.

## Colors

### Architectural Palette Strategy
The color system creates a neutral, warm background that guarantees true color perception during exercises:

- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base substrate is `#F6F4EF` (warm vellum/paper). Cards, toolbars, and test viewports sit upon crisp `#FFFFFF` to provide maximum local contrast and pristine optical evaluation.
- **Typography & Structural Contrast**: `#1C1C1A` serves as the primary ink color, providing sharp contrast without the harsh artificial glare of pure `#000000`. Muted functional text and secondary metadata rely on `#62625D`.
- **System Accent**: `#4D46E8` (electric cobalt/indigo) is strictly reserved for primary operational directives: active handles, focal CTA buttons, and interactive keyboard focus rings.
- **Domain Thematics**: Four distinct chromatic tokens signify operational disciplines without competing with the interface:
  - `domain-typography` (`#F2B8CE`): Typography, kerning, and baseline alignment.
  - `domain-color` (`#D8EF71`): Hue discrimination, saturation matching, and contrast audits.
  - `domain-composition` (`#B9D9FF`): Spacing, hierarchy, and spatial balance.
  - `domain-vector` (`#FFBC8A`): Bézier geometry, path curvature, and node precision.

### Strict Contrast & Usage Rules
All category badges pair their domain color directly with `#1C1C1A` body ink. System feedback must remain chromatic-pure: avoid overlaying tinted semi-transparent washes across active observation areas to prevent altering the user's color perception during training.

## Typography

### Typographic Triad
The system deploys three complementary typographic voices:

1. **The Editorial Headline (`Newsreader`)**: A classic serif with open counters and sharp terminal cuts. It handles landing heroes, major challenge titles, and typographic specimen reveals. It signals cultured connoisseurship and sets an editorial studio tone.
2. **The Rational Interface (`Manrope`)**: A geometric grotesque sans-serif with open apertures and neutral cadence. It renders exercise prompts, explanations, form inputs, button labels, and system navigations with legibility.
3. **The Instrument Metric (`Space Mono`)**: A monospaced typeface dedicated exclusively to visual readouts: Bézier anchor coordinates, kerning deltas in em-fractions, CIE-L*a*b* coordinates, and precision stopwatches.

### Text Setting Guidance
- **Line Length**: Body text is capped at 65 characters to preserve scanning comfort.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: All values that change dynamically in the UI (offsets, point counts, scores) must use `mono-metric` to prevent layout shift.
- **Eyebrows & Overlines**: Use `label-caps` in uppercase styling with `0.08em` tracking for subheadings and card badges.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The canvas structure balances editorial balance on desktop with surgical workspace efficiency on mobile:
- **Desktop (1440px target)**: A 12-column layout flanked by generous `3rem` margins. The workspace features deliberate asymmetry: primary challenge viewports occupy 8 columns to maximize the manipulation area, while contextual instructions and live instrument dials share a dedicated 4-column sidebar.
- **Mobile (390px target)**: A single-column vertical flow with unified `1.25rem` margins. The interface enforces a strict top-to-bottom hierarchy: Target Visual &rarr; Current Challenge Canvas &rarr; Monospaced Telemetry &rarr; Manipulation Handles &rarr; Affirmative CTA.

### Spacing Scale & Rhythms
The rhythm is built on an incremental base-4 system:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between telemetry tags and monospaced labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal padding for tags, badge containers, and compact tool buttons.
- `space-md` (16px): Input field internal padding, component margins, and card headers.
- `space-lg` (24px): Padding within panels, workbench cards, and form field stacks.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks, modal internal layouts, and workspace-to-control separation.

## Elevation & Depth

### Structural Elevation Strategy
This design system rejects heavy drop shadows, diffused neon glows, and glassmorphism. Elevation is communicated through three structural devices:

1. **Surface Tone Contrasts**:
   - Level 0 (Base Ground): Canvas `#F6F4EF`.
   - Level 1 (Workbench / Canvas Cards): Crisp opaque `#FFFFFF`.
   - Level 2 (Flyout Inspections & Modals): Pristine `#FFFFFF` paired with an outline boundary.

2. **Hairline Outlines (`Low-Contrast Outlines`)**:
   Every floating card, inspection HUD, and input container is bounded by a crisp 1px solid border rendered in `border-subtle` (`#DCDAD3`).

3. **Restrained Utility Shadow**:
   For floating modals and transient dropdown tooltips, a single minimal shadow is permitted:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(28, 28, 26, 0.06), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(28, 28, 26, 0.04)`.
   This mimics the subtle cast shadow of heavy stock paper resting flat on an atelier table.

## Shapes

### Edge Philosophy
The shape language uses structured soft corners:
- Standard UI containers (cards, manipulation areas, action panels, and dialogs) use a subtle radius of **4px** (`0.25rem`).
- Large dialog surfaces and workspace viewports scale to **8px** (`0.5rem`).
- Full pill rounding (`9999px`) is prohibited on cards, viewports, and primary structural containers. It is reserved solely for interactive circular nodes, vector curve Bézier control handles, and small metric pills.
- Hairlines are consistently 1px solid, creating a crisp and structured drafting-board aesthetic.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#4D46E8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a subtle darkening (`#3E37D1`) without translation. Active state scales down to `0.98`. Focus ring uses a 2px offset solid `#4D46E8` outline.
- **Secondary (Studio Outline)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px solid `#DCDAD3` border, `#1C1C1A` text. Hover shifts the background to `#F6F4EF` and border to `#1C1C1A`.
- **Tertiary / Utility**: Ghost surface, `#62625D` text, transitioning to `#1C1C1A` with an underlined hover state.
- **Min Hit Target**: Enforced `44px` height across all touch viewports.

### 2. Domain Chips & Badges
- Compact indicators pairing the domain color (`#F2B8CE`, `#D8EF71`, `#B9D9FF`, `#FFBC8A`) as a solid background with `#1C1C1A` text set in `label-caps`. 
- Finished with 2px corner radii, a subtle 1px inset border (`rgba(28, 28, 26, 0.08)`), and 4px vertical / 8px horizontal padding.

### 3. Cards & Exercise Plates
- `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px solid `#DCDAD3` frame with 4px corner rounding.
- Divided internally by 1px horizontal `#DCDAD3` rules separating the exercise preview, descriptive header, and footer telemetry.
- On hover, cards transition their border color from `#DCDAD3` to `#1C1C1A` over 150ms.

### 4. Interactive Inputs & Calibration Sliders
- **Text Inputs**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#DCDAD3`, `#1C1C1A` typed value, placeholder text in `#62625D`. Focus state transitions the border to `#4D46E8` with a 1px glow.
- **Precision Sliders (Color, Spacing, Scale)**:
  - Track: 2px solid hairline in `#DCDAD3`.
  - Thumb: A 16px circular puck finished in `#FFFFFF` with a 2px solid `#1C1C1A` rim.
  - Active drag state expands the thumb slightly and displays a floating `Space Mono` tooltip readout overhead.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Checkbox**: 18px square box with 2px border radius, 1px solid `#DCDAD3`, resting on `#FFFFFF`. Checked state fills the box with `#1C1C1A` displaying a clean white geometric check glyph.
- **Radio Buttons**: 18px round border with 1px solid `#DCDAD3`. Selected state features an interior `#1C1C1A` bullet.

### 6. Atelier-Specific Elements
- **Bézier Path Handles**: 8px circular anchor nodes `#FFFFFF` with a 1.5px `#4D46E8` perimeter; handle stems are 1px solid `#4D46E8`.
- **Optical Ruler & Spacing Guides**: 1px dashed or solid markers in `#4D46E8` accompanied by numeric floating callouts rendered in `mono-sm`.
- **Comparison Splitter (Before / After)**: A centered 1px hairline dividing two canvases, with a 28px circular dragger handle set in `#1C1C1A` carrying dual opposing arrow vectors.
